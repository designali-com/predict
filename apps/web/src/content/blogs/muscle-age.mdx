# What Is MuscleAGE?

Most people believe muscle health depends on age, exercise, or body weight. In reality, your muscles can age much faster—or slower—than your birth certificate suggests.

**MuscleAGE** is an advanced biological assessment that measures the true health of your muscles using blood biomarkers, artificial intelligence, and clinical data. Instead of waiting until pain becomes severe, MuscleAGE identifies the biological changes happening inside your body long before symptoms appear.

Whether you're an athlete, office worker, or someone experiencing recurring joint pain, MuscleAGE provides a personalized understanding of how healthy your muscles really are.

---

## Why Chronological Age Doesn't Tell the Whole Story

Two people may both be 45 years old but have completely different muscle health.

One may:

- Walk comfortably every day
- Recover quickly from exercise
- Have strong muscles
- Experience little inflammation

The other may experience:

- Knee pain
- Back pain
- Muscle weakness
- Slow recovery
- Chronic inflammation

Although their chronological age is identical, their **biological muscle age** may differ by 15–20 years.

That's what MuscleAGE measures.

---

## What Is Biological Muscle Age?

Biological Muscle Age represents how healthy your muscles, joints, connective tissues, and inflammatory systems are compared with healthy individuals.

Unlike traditional health checkups that simply report whether a value is "normal," MuscleAGE evaluates multiple biological systems together to estimate how your muscles are actually aging.

This allows clinicians to identify hidden degeneration before permanent damage occurs.

---

## Why Muscle Health Matters

Healthy muscles do much more than help you move.

They are responsible for:

- Supporting joints
- Improving balance
- Preventing injuries
- Regulating metabolism
- Maintaining posture
- Protecting bones
- Reducing inflammation
- Improving recovery

When muscle quality declines, almost every movement becomes more difficult.

---

## Common Signs Your MuscleAGE May Be Higher Than Your Actual Age

You may benefit from MuscleAGE testing if you experience:

- Persistent knee pain
- Frequent back pain
- Shoulder stiffness
- Neck pain
- Muscle weakness
- Slow exercise recovery
- Difficulty climbing stairs
- Joint stiffness
- Reduced flexibility
- Fatigue after light activity

These symptoms often develop gradually, making early biological testing especially valuable.

## How MuscleAGE Works

The process combines laboratory biomarkers with AI-powered analysis.

### Step 1 — Blood Sample

A blood sample is collected to measure biomarkers associated with inflammation, muscle health, bone metabolism, and tissue repair.

---

### Step 2 — Biomarker Analysis

Multiple biological markers are analyzed simultaneously rather than independently.

Examples include:

- hs-CRP
- IL-6
- COMP
- CTX-II
- Vitamin D
- Creatinine
- MMP-9
- Osteocalcin
- BDNF
- VEGF

Each biomarker contributes unique information about your musculoskeletal health.

---

### Step 3 — AI Interpretation

Artificial intelligence evaluates relationships between biomarkers instead of viewing them separately.

This provides a much more comprehensive assessment of biological muscle health.

---

### Step 4 — MuscleAGE Score

A personalized MuscleAGE score is generated, showing whether your muscles are biologically younger or older than your chronological age.

---

### Step 5 — Personalized Recovery Plan

Based on your results, healthcare professionals create a customized treatment strategy designed specifically for your biology.

Recommendations may include:

- Nutrition optimization
- Targeted supplementation
- Exercise programming
- Recovery strategies
- Lifestyle modifications
- Biomarker monitoring

# Biomarkers Used in MuscleAGE

## hs-CRP

Measures systemic inflammation.

Higher levels often indicate chronic inflammatory activity associated with joint degeneration and metabolic dysfunction.

---

## IL-6

A major inflammatory cytokine involved in chronic pain, immune response, and muscle recovery.

Persistently elevated IL-6 may indicate ongoing tissue inflammation.

---

## COMP

Cartilage Oligomeric Matrix Protein is an important marker of cartilage breakdown.

Higher values may suggest increased joint degeneration.

---

## CTX-II

A biomarker reflecting cartilage degradation.

Frequently evaluated in osteoarthritis research.

---

## Vitamin D

Supports muscle strength, immune regulation, and bone health.

Deficiency is commonly associated with muscle weakness.

---

## Creatinine

Provides insight into muscle metabolism and kidney function.

---

## MMP-9

Associated with tissue remodeling and extracellular matrix degradation.

Elevated levels may indicate ongoing tissue damage.

---

## Osteocalcin

A marker related to bone formation and skeletal health.

---

## BDNF

Supports nerve health, muscle function, and cognitive performance.

---

## VEGF

Plays an important role in blood vessel formation and tissue healing.

# Frequently Asked Questions

## Is MuscleAGE the same as body age?

No.

Body age estimates overall health, while MuscleAGE specifically evaluates biological muscle and musculoskeletal health using biomarkers.

---

## Does MuscleAGE replace MRI or X-rays?

No.

Imaging evaluates structural damage, while MuscleAGE measures biological changes that may occur before structural abnormalities become visible.

---

## Can MuscleAGE improve over time?

Yes.

With appropriate nutrition, exercise, medical guidance, and lifestyle changes, biomarker profiles may improve, leading to a healthier biological muscle age.

---

## Is MuscleAGE useful even if I don't have pain?

Yes.

Many biological changes occur years before symptoms become noticeable.

Early detection provides an opportunity for preventive intervention.
