PREDICT helps people understand their biology. We turn biomarker science into results people can read, trust, and act on. The brand should feel the same: clear, precise, and calm.

## Principles

- **Clear** — Say one thing at a time. Plenty of white space, few colours, plain words.
- **Precise** — Health data is exact, so the brand is too. Align things, use real units, and never round away meaning.
- **Calm** — Results can be worrying. Inform without alarming, and save strong colour for what needs attention.
- **Human** — Science, explained by people. The mascot and the voice keep it warm.

## Anatomy

The logo has two parts: the **wordmark**, PREDICT in custom letterforms, and the **mark**, two strokes above the T. The mark also works alone as the icon. Never separate the wordmark from the mark, or change their size or position relative to each other.

## Clear space

Keep space around the logo free of text, images, and edges. The minimum clear space is **x**, the height of the P, on every side. More space is always better.

## Colour versions

The logo comes in one colour at a time. Use black on light backgrounds and white on dark ones. White on Predict Purple and purple on white are for brand moments, such as campaigns and covers.

### Status colours

Green, yellow, blue, orange, and red are kept for biomarker status, from normal to extreme. Don't use them for decoration or for the brand. See [Colors](/docs/design/colors) for the full list.

## Typography

**Neue Montreal** is the brand typeface, for everything from headlines to buttons. **Geist Mono** is for codes, IDs, and numbers that need to line up.

<BrandTypography />

- Use Medium (500) for headings and Regular (400) for body text. Keep Light and Bold for rare emphasis.
- Use sentence case for headings and buttons: "Book a test", not "Book A Test".
- Use tabular numbers (`tabular-nums`) for results, so columns line up.
- See [Typography](/docs/design/typography) for the type scale.

## Voice and tone

Write the way a good clinician talks: plainly, kindly, and exactly.

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| Your LDL-C is above the healthy range. | DANGER! Your cholesterol is critical! |
| Fast for 10 to 12 hours before your test. | Please ensure adequate fasting prior to phlebotomy. |
| 3 results need attention. | You failed 3 tests. |
| Discuss these results with your clinician. | You have heart disease. |
| Book a test | Click here to book now!!! |

- Use plain words. Explain a term the first time, such as "hs-CRP, a marker of inflammation".
- Lead with what the result means, then the number.
- Never diagnose. Results are information, not medical advice.
- Use "you" and "your". Avoid jargon, exclamation marks, and all caps.

## In code

The logo, icon, and mascot are React components in `@workspace/ui`. Use them instead of image files so they follow the theme.

```tsx
import { Logo, LogoIcon } from "@workspace/ui/predict/logo"
import { PredictMascot } from "@workspace/ui/predict/predict-mascot"

<Logo role="img" aria-label="PREDICT" className="h-6 w-auto" />
```

The brand colours are theme tokens: `bg-primary` for Predict Purple, `bg-secondary` for lilac, and `bg-chart-1` to `bg-chart-5` for the purple scale. See [Logo](/docs/ui/logo) for the full component reference.
