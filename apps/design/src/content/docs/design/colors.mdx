### Usage Guidelines

- Use severity colors consistently across biomarker cards, reports, and health indicators.
- Use semantic Tailwind tokens instead of hardcoding colors in application components.
- Maintain accessible text contrast on every color surface.
- Keep light and dark themes consistent with the global CSS variables.
