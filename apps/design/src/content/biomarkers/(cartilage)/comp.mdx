## What it measures

Cartilage oligomeric matrix protein helps hold the collagen in cartilage together. It is released into the blood as cartilage breaks down.

The mean is `(low + high) / 2` and the SD is `(high − low) / 4`, so the healthy range covers Z from −2 to +2.

## Reading the result

- **High** — Points to cartilage breakdown, as in osteoarthritis or joint injury. It can rise after hard joint loading.
- **Low** — Not a concern.

## Notes

- Avoid running or heavy joint loading in the hours before the test.
- This page is general information, not medical advice. Discuss results with a clinician.
