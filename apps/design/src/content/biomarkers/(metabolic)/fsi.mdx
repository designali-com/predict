## What it measures

The level of insulin in the blood after at least 8 hours without food. When the body resists insulin, the pancreas makes more to keep glucose normal.

## Reference range

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Abbreviation | FSI |
| Group | Glucose and insulin |
| Unit | µIU·mL⁻¹ |
| Healthy range | 2–20 |
| Mean | 11 |
| SD | 4.5 |
| Direction | ↑ Bad — a raised value is the concern. |

The mean is `(low + high) / 2` and the SD is `(high − low) / 4`, so the healthy range covers Z from −2 to +2.

## Reading the result

- **High** — Points to insulin resistance, often before blood glucose rises.
- **Low** — Not a concern in this panel. Very low insulin with high glucose can point to diabetes and needs a doctor's review.

## Notes

- Combine with fasting glucose to work out HOMA-IR, a measure of insulin resistance.
- Insulin assays vary between labs.
- This page is general information, not medical advice. Discuss results with a clinician.
