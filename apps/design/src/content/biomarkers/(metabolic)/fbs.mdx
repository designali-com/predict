## What it measures

The level of glucose in the blood after at least 8 hours without food. It shows how well the body keeps glucose steady between meals.

## Reference range

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Abbreviation | FBS |
| Group | Glucose and insulin |
| Unit | mg·dL⁻¹ |
| Healthy range | 70–99 |
| Mean | 84.5 |
| SD | 7.25 |
| Direction | ↑ Bad — a raised value is the concern. |

The mean is `(low + high) / 2` and the SD is `(high − low) / 4`, so the healthy range covers Z from −2 to +2.

## Reading the result

- **High** — 100 to 125 mg·dL⁻¹ suggests prediabetes and 126 or more on two tests suggests diabetes.
- **Low** — Not the concern this panel tracks. Values below 70 mg·dL⁻¹ are hypoglycaemia and can cause symptoms.

## Notes

- To convert to mmol·L⁻¹, divide by 18.
- Illness, stress, and some medicines raise it.
- This page is general information, not medical advice. Discuss results with a clinician.
