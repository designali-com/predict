## What it measures

Type II collagen is the main structural protein in joint cartilage. CTX-II is a piece released when it breaks down, so it tracks cartilage loss.

The mean is `(low + high) / 2` and the SD is `(high − low) / 4`, so the healthy range covers Z from −2 to +2.

## Reading the result

- **High** — Points to faster cartilage breakdown, as in osteoarthritis.
- **Low** — Not a concern.

## Notes

- Often measured in urine. These values are for serum.
- Results vary with time of day, so test at the same time each time.
- This page is general information, not medical advice. Discuss results with a clinician.
