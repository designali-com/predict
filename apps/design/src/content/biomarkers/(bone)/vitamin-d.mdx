## What it measures

25-hydroxyvitamin D is made in the liver from vitamin D from sunlight, food, and supplements. It is the stored form the body draws on.

The mean is `(low + high) / 2` and the SD is `(high − low) / 4`, so the healthy range covers Z from −2 to +2.

## Reading the result

- **High** — Not the concern this panel tracks. Very high values, above about 150 ng·mL⁻¹, usually come from too many supplements and can raise calcium.
- **Low** — Points to vitamin D insufficiency. It is linked to weaker bones, muscle weakness, and a raised PTH.

## Notes

- To convert to nmol·L⁻¹, multiply by 2.5.
- Levels are lower in winter and with darker skin, less sun, or obesity.
- This page is general information, not medical advice. Discuss results with a clinician.
