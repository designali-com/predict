## What it measures

Parathyroid hormone is released by the parathyroid glands when calcium falls. It frees calcium from bone, keeps it in the kidneys, and activates vitamin D.

The mean is `(low + high) / 2` and the SD is `(high − low) / 4`, so the healthy range covers Z from −2 to +2.

## Reading the result

- **High** — Often a response to low vitamin D or low calcium intake. It can also point to an overactive parathyroid gland or kidney disease. Long-term high PTH thins bone.
- **Low** — Not the concern this panel tracks. Low PTH with high calcium needs a doctor's review.

## Notes

- Read it together with calcium and vitamin D.
- This page is general information, not medical advice. Discuss results with a clinician.
