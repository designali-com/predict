## What it measures

Matrix metalloproteinase-9 breaks down collagen and other parts of the matrix that holds tissue together. Immune cells release it during inflammation and repair.

## Reading the result

- **High** — Suggests active tissue breakdown, as in joint, bone, or blood vessel inflammation. It is linked to unstable plaque in arteries.
- **Low** — Not a concern.

## Notes

- Serum and plasma values differ, because blood cells release MMP-9 as a sample clots. Compare like with like.
- This page is general information, not medical advice. Discuss results with a clinician.
