## What it measures

Procollagen type I N-terminal propeptide is cut off as osteoblasts lay down new type I collagen, the main protein in bone. Its level tracks how much new bone is being built.

## Reading the result

- **High** — Not the concern this panel tracks. High values can mean fast bone turnover, healing fractures, or bone disease.
- **Low** — Suggests slow bone formation, with a higher risk of bone loss.

## Notes

- Often used to check the response to osteoporosis treatment.
