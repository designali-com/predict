---
title: Composite Biological LegAge Index
description: "A weighted score of inflammation, vessel, musculoskeletal, bone, nerve, cardiometabolic, and kidney function in the legs."
---

The CBLAI sums seven domains of leg ageing into one score. It uses 25 biomarkers, the most of any index. A higher score means more of the panel sits outside its healthy range, with the most weight on the domains that matter most for this index.

See [Indices](/biomarkers/indices) for how every index is calculated.

## Domains

| Domain | Biomarkers | αd |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inflammation Risk Factor (IRF) | [hs-CRP](/biomarkers/hs-crp), [NLR](/biomarkers/nlr), [IL-6](/biomarkers/il-6), [MDA](/biomarkers/mda) | 2 |
| Vascular & Angiogenic Function (VAF) | [VEGF](/biomarkers/vegf), [MMP-9](/biomarkers/mmp-9), [TGF-β1](/biomarkers/tgf-beta1) | 2 |
| Musculoskeletal Health Factor (MHF) | [COMP](/biomarkers/comp), [CK-MM](/biomarkers/ck-mm), [Aldolase A](/biomarkers/aldolase-a), [CTX-II](/biomarkers/ctx-ii) | 2 |
| Bone Metabolic Function (BMF) | [PTH](/biomarkers/pth), [Ca/P ratio](/biomarkers/ca-p-ratio), [Vitamin D](/biomarkers/vitamin-d), [Osteocalcin](/biomarkers/osteocalcin) | 1 |
| Peripheral Nerve Function (PNF) | [BDNF](/biomarkers/bdnf), [Substance P](/biomarkers/substance-p) | 1 |
| Cardiometabolic Function (CMF) | [HbA1c](/biomarkers/hba1c), [TC](/biomarkers/total-cholesterol), [LDL-C](/biomarkers/ldl-c), [HDL-C](/biomarkers/hdl-c), [TG](/biomarkers/triglycerides) | 6 |
| Renal Filtration Factor (RFF) | [Creatinine](/biomarkers/creatinine), [BUN](/biomarkers/bun), [eGFR](/biomarkers/egfr) | 3 |

αd is the fixed priority of each domain in this index.

The example patient's CBLAI is **11.11**.

Cardiometabolic Function adds the most to the score (6.39 of 11.11).

## Notes

- The mean and SD come from the healthy range, so a result inside the range has a |Z| of 2 or less.
- The score has no fixed upper limit. Compare it over time for the same person, not between indices.
- This page is general information, not medical advice. Discuss results with a clinician.
