---
title: Composite Biological Venous Aging Index
description: "A weighted score of inflammation, fibrotic remodelling, endothelial dysfunction, and vein wall remodelling."
---

The CBVAI sums four domains of venous ageing into one score. It uses 10 biomarkers. A higher score means more of the panel sits outside its healthy range, with the most weight on the domains that matter most for this index.

See [Indices](/biomarkers/indices) for how every index is calculated.

## Domains

| Domain | Biomarkers | αd |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inflammation Burden (IB) | [hs-CRP](/biomarkers/hs-crp), [NLR](/biomarkers/nlr), [IL-6](/biomarkers/il-6), [TNF-α](/biomarkers/tnf-alpha) | 4 |
| Fibro Remodeling (FR) | [TGF-β1](/biomarkers/tgf-beta1), [PINP](/biomarkers/pinp), [TIMP-1](/biomarkers/timp-1) | 4 |
| Endothelial Dysfunction (ED) | [VEGF](/biomarkers/vegf), [ET-1](/biomarkers/et-1) | 6 |
| Vascular Wall Remodeling (VWR) | [MMP-9](/biomarkers/mmp-9) | 1 |

αd is the fixed priority of each domain in this index.

## Example

These are the results of one example patient, worked through step by step.

### Biomarker Z-scores

Each result becomes a Z-score: `(value − mean) / SD`, with the mean and SD taken from the healthy range.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| [NLR](/biomarkers/nlr) | 1.0–3.0 ratio | 2 | 0.5 | ↑ Bad | 3.2 | 2.40 | 2.40 |
| [IL-6](/biomarkers/il-6) | 0.5–7.0 pg·mL⁻¹ | 3.75 | 1.63 | ↑ Bad | 9.8 | 3.71 | 3.71 |
| [TNF-α](/biomarkers/tnf-alpha) | 1.0–8.0 pg·mL⁻¹ | 4.5 | 1.75 | ↑ Bad | 12.7 | 4.69 | 4.69 |

### Domain scores

| Domain | Mean \|Z\|d | Wd | Mean \|Z\|d × Wd | αd | Score |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| IB | 3.38 | 0.26 | 0.89 | 4 | 3.55 |
| FR | 3.17 | 0.25 | 0.78 | 4 | 3.13 |
| ED | 3.77 | 0.29 | 1.11 | 6 | 6.63 |
| VWR | 2.53 | 0.20 | 0.50 | 1 | 0.50 |
| **Total** | **12.84** | **1.00** | **3.27** | | **13.81** |

The example patient's CBVAI is **13.81**.

Endothelial Dysfunction adds the most to the score (6.63 of 13.81).

## Notes

- The mean and SD come from the healthy range, so a result inside the range has a |Z| of 2 or less.
- The score has no fixed upper limit. Compare it over time for the same person, not between indices.
- This page is general information, not medical advice. Discuss results with a clinician.
