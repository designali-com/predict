---
title: Composite Biological OrthoAge Index
description: "A weighted score of how far inflammation, mineral regulation, bone and cartilage output, muscle breakdown, and nerve and vessel coupling have aged."
---

The CBOAI sums five domains of orthopaedic ageing into one score. It uses 15 biomarkers. A higher score means more of the panel sits outside its healthy range, with the most weight on the domains that matter most for this index.

See [Indices](/biomarkers/indices) for how every index is calculated.

## Domains

| Domain | Biomarkers | αd |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inflammation & Immune Aging (IIA) | [hs-CRP](/biomarkers/hs-crp), [NLR](/biomarkers/nlr), [IL-6](/biomarkers/il-6) | 6 |
| Endocrine-Mineral Regulation (EMR) | [PTH](/biomarkers/pth), [Ca/P ratio](/biomarkers/ca-p-ratio), [Vitamin D](/biomarkers/vitamin-d) | 1 |
| Bone & Cartilage Output (BCO) | [PINP](/biomarkers/pinp), [CTX-II](/biomarkers/ctx-ii), [Osteocalcin](/biomarkers/osteocalcin) | 1 |
| Muscle-ECM Degeneration (M-ECMD) | [CK-MM](/biomarkers/ck-mm), [Aldolase A](/biomarkers/aldolase-a), [MMP-9](/biomarkers/mmp-9), [MDA](/biomarkers/mda) | 5 |
| Neurovascular Coupling (NVC) | [VEGF](/biomarkers/vegf), [BDNF](/biomarkers/bdnf) | 3 |

αd is the fixed priority of each domain in this index.

The example patient's CBOAI is **9.42**.

Inflammation & Immune Aging adds the most to the score (3.82 of 9.42).

## Notes

- The mean and SD come from the healthy range, so a result inside the range has a |Z| of 2 or less.
- The score has no fixed upper limit. Compare it over time for the same person, not between indices.
- This page is general information, not medical advice. Discuss results with a clinician.
