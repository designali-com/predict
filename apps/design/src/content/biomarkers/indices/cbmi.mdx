---
title: Composite Biological Musculoskeletal Index
description: "A weighted score of inflammation, muscle and cartilage breakdown, bone turnover, cardiometabolic, and nerve and vessel risk."
---

The CBMI sums five domains of musculoskeletal risk into one score. It uses 21 biomarkers. A higher score means more of the panel sits outside its healthy range, with the most weight on the domains that matter most for this index.

See [Indices](/biomarkers/indices) for how every index is calculated.

## Domains

| Domain | Biomarkers | αd |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inflammatory Risk (IR) | [hs-CRP](/biomarkers/hs-crp), [NLR](/biomarkers/nlr), [IL-6](/biomarkers/il-6) | 2 |
| Muscle-Cartilage Degeneration Risk (MCDR) | [CK-MM](/biomarkers/ck-mm), [Aldolase A](/biomarkers/aldolase-a), [IGF-1](/biomarkers/igf-1), [Myostatin](/biomarkers/myostatin), [CTX-II](/biomarkers/ctx-ii) | 2 |
| Bone Turnover Risk (BTR) | [PTH](/biomarkers/pth), [Ca/P ratio](/biomarkers/ca-p-ratio), [Vitamin D](/biomarkers/vitamin-d), [Osteocalcin](/biomarkers/osteocalcin), [PINP](/biomarkers/pinp) | 1 |
| Cardiometabolic Risk (CMR) | [HbA1c](/biomarkers/hba1c), [TC](/biomarkers/total-cholesterol), [LDL-C](/biomarkers/ldl-c), [HDL-C](/biomarkers/hdl-c) | 6 |
| Neurovascular Function Risk (NVFR) | [BDNF](/biomarkers/bdnf), [VEGF](/biomarkers/vegf), [NOx](/biomarkers/nox) | 1 |

αd is the fixed priority of each domain in this index.

The example patient's CBMI is **13.79**.

Cardiometabolic Risk adds the most to the score (10.91 of 13.79).

## Notes

- The mean and SD come from the healthy range, so a result inside the range has a |Z| of 2 or less.
- The score has no fixed upper limit. Compare it over time for the same person, not between indices.
- This page is general information, not medical advice. Discuss results with a clinician.
