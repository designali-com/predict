---
title: Composite Biological Neurofunction Index
description: "A weighted score of neuroinflammation, muscle damage, oxidative stress, neurotrophic support, and glial activation."
---

The CBNI sums five domains of nerve function into one score. It uses 11 biomarkers. A higher score means more of the panel sits outside its healthy range, with the most weight on the domains that matter most for this index.

See [Indices](/biomarkers/indices) for how every index is calculated.

## Domains

| Domain | Biomarkers | αd |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Neuroinflammatory Activation (NIA) | [hs-CRP](/biomarkers/hs-crp), [NLR](/biomarkers/nlr), [IL-6](/biomarkers/il-6) | 3 |
| Muscle Damage & Matrix Remodeling (MDMR) | [CK-MM](/biomarkers/ck-mm), [Aldolase A](/biomarkers/aldolase-a), [MMP-9](/biomarkers/mmp-9) | 2 |
| Oxidative Stress & Cellular Damage (OSCD) | [MDA](/biomarkers/mda) | 6 |
| Neurotrophic Regulation (NTR) | [BDNF](/biomarkers/bdnf), [NGF](/biomarkers/ngf) | 1 |
| Neuroglial Activation (NGA) | [Substance P](/biomarkers/substance-p), [GFAP](/biomarkers/gfap) | 2 |

αd is the fixed priority of each domain in this index.

The example patient's CBNI is **10.04**.

Oxidative Stress & Cellular Damage adds the most to the score (6.00 of 10.04).

## Notes

- The mean and SD come from the healthy range, so a result inside the range has a |Z| of 2 or less.
- The score has no fixed upper limit. Compare it over time for the same person, not between indices.
- This page is general information, not medical advice. Discuss results with a clinician.
