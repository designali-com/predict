## What it measures

Urea is made in the liver from the ammonia left when protein is broken down, and cleared by the kidneys. Some labs report urea and others BUN.

The mean is `(low + high) / 2` and the SD is `(high − low) / 4`, so the healthy range covers Z from −2 to +2.

## Reading the result

- **High** — Can mean reduced kidney function, dehydration, or a high-protein diet.
- **Low** — Not the concern this panel tracks. Low values can come from a low-protein diet or liver disease.

## Notes

- To turn urea into BUN, multiply by 0.467.
- This page is general information, not medical advice. Discuss results with a clinician.
