## What it measures

Albumin is made by the liver. It holds fluid in the blood vessels and carries hormones, medicines, and minerals.

## Reference range

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Abbreviation | ALB |
| Group | Kidney and protein |
| Unit | g·dL⁻¹ |
| Healthy range | 3.5–5 |
| Mean | 4.25 |
| SD | 0.375 |
| Direction | ↓ Bad — a lowered value is the concern. |

The mean is `(low + high) / 2` and the SD is `(high − low) / 4`, so the healthy range covers Z from −2 to +2.

## Reading the result

- **High** — Not the concern this panel tracks. High values usually mean dehydration.
- **Low** — Can mean poor nutrition, liver disease, kidney loss of protein, or inflammation. It is linked to frailty and poorer outcomes.

## Notes

- Albumin falls during inflammation, even with good nutrition.
- This page is general information, not medical advice. Discuss results with a clinician.
