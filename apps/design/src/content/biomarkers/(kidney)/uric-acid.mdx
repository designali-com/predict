## What it measures

Uric acid forms when the body breaks down purines, from its own cells and from food. The kidneys clear most of it.

The mean is `(low + high) / 2` and the SD is `(high − low) / 4`, so the healthy range covers Z from −2 to +2.

## Reading the result

- **High** — Raises the risk of gout and kidney stones. It is linked to high blood pressure, insulin resistance, and kidney disease.
- **Low** — Not a concern.

## Notes

- Alcohol, sugary drinks, and red meat raise it.
- Ranges are lower for women before menopause.
- This page is general information, not medical advice. Discuss results with a clinician.
