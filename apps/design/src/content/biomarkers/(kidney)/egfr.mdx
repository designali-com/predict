## What it measures

Estimated glomerular filtration rate is calculated from serum creatinine, age, and sex. It estimates how much blood the kidneys clean each minute.

## Reference range

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Abbreviation | eGFR |
| Group | Kidney and protein |
| Unit | mL·min⁻¹·1.73 m⁻² |
| Healthy range | 90–120 |
| Mean | 105 |
| SD | 7.5 |
| Direction | ↓ Bad — a lowered value is the concern. |

The mean is `(low + high) / 2` and the SD is `(high − low) / 4`, so the healthy range covers Z from −2 to +2.

## Reading the result

- **High** — Not a concern.
- **Low** — Suggests reduced kidney function. 60 to 89 is mildly reduced, and below 60 for three months or more is chronic kidney disease.

## Notes

- eGFR falls slowly with age.
- Muscle mass affects creatinine, so the estimate is less accurate in very muscular or frail people.
- This page is general information, not medical advice. Discuss results with a clinician.
