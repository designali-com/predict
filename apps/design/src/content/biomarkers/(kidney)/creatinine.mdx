## What it measures

Creatinine comes from the normal breakdown of muscle creatine and is cleared by the kidneys. When filtering falls, it builds up.

## Reference range

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Abbreviation | Cr |
| Group | Kidney and protein |
| Unit | mg·dL⁻¹ |
| Healthy range | 0.7–1.3 |
| Mean | 1 |
| SD | 0.15 |
| Direction | ↑ Bad — a raised value is the concern. |

The mean is `(low + high) / 2` and the SD is `(high − low) / 4`, so the healthy range covers Z from −2 to +2.

## Reading the result

- **High** — Suggests reduced kidney function. High muscle mass, a large meat meal, or dehydration can also raise it.
- **Low** — Not a concern. Low values can reflect low muscle mass.

## Notes

- Ranges differ for men and women.
- Creatine supplements raise it.
- This page is general information, not medical advice. Discuss results with a clinician.
