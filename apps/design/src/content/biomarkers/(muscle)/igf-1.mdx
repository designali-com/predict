## What it measures

Insulin-like growth factor-1 is made mainly by the liver in response to growth hormone. It drives tissue growth and repair, including muscle protein synthesis.

## Reading the result

- **High** — Not the concern this panel tracks. Very high values can point to excess growth hormone and need a doctor's review.
- **Low** — Can reflect poor nutrition, low growth hormone, liver disease, or ageing. Low IGF-1 is linked to muscle loss and frailty.

## Notes

- IGF-1 falls naturally with age, so age-specific ranges are more accurate.
- This page is general information, not medical advice. Discuss results with a clinician.
