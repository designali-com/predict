## What it measures

Aldolase A is an enzyme in the glycolysis pathway, most abundant in skeletal muscle. It leaks into the blood when muscle cells are damaged.

## Reading the result

- **High** — Suggests muscle injury or inflammation. It can rise in some muscle diseases even when CK is normal.
- **Low** — Not a concern.

## Notes

- Like CK, it rises after strenuous exercise.
- Liver injury and haemolysis can also raise it.
- This page is general information, not medical advice. Discuss results with a clinician.
