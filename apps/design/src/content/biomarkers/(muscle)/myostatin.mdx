## What it measures

Myostatin is made by muscle cells and acts as a brake on muscle growth. Higher levels mean a stronger signal to hold back or break down muscle.

## Reading the result

- **High** — Linked to muscle loss with ageing, inactivity, and chronic disease.
- **Low** — Not a concern.

## Notes

- Assays differ a lot. Compare values from the same lab.
- Resistance training tends to lower it.
- This page is general information, not medical advice. Discuss results with a clinician.
