## What it measures

Nitric oxide relaxes blood vessels and keeps their lining healthy. It breaks down quickly into nitrate and nitrite, which are measured instead.

The mean is `(low + high) / 2` and the SD is `(high − low) / 4`, so the healthy range covers Z from −2 to +2.

## Reading the result

- **High** — Not the concern this panel tracks. High values can come from a diet rich in nitrate or from inflammation.
- **Low** — Suggests less nitric oxide and poorer blood vessel function.

## Notes

- Food, especially leafy greens and beetroot, raises it. Test fasting.
- This page is general information, not medical advice. Discuss results with a clinician.
