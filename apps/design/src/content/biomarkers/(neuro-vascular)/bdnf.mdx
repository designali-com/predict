## What it measures

Brain-derived neurotrophic factor helps nerve cells survive, grow, and form new connections. Most in the blood is stored in platelets.

## Reference range

| Healthy range | 15–40 |
| Mean | 27.5 |
| SD | 6.25 |
| Direction | ↓ Bad — a lowered value is the concern. |

The mean is `(low + high) / 2` and the SD is `(high − low) / 4`, so the healthy range covers Z from −2 to +2.

## Reading the result

- **High** — Not the concern this panel tracks.
- **Low** — Linked to depression, cognitive decline, and poor metabolic health.

## Notes

- Serum values are much higher than plasma values, because platelets release BDNF as a sample clots.
- Exercise tends to raise it.
- This page is general information, not medical advice. Discuss results with a clinician.
