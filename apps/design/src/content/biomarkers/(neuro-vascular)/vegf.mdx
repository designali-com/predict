## What it measures

Vascular endothelial growth factor tells the body to grow new blood vessels. It matters for healing and for supplying exercising muscle, but also feeds disease when too active.

The mean is `(low + high) / 2` and the SD is `(high − low) / 4`, so the healthy range covers Z from −2 to +2.

## Reading the result

- **High** — Can reflect healing or a training response, but also tissue with too little oxygen, inflammation, or tumour growth.
- **Low** — Can mean poor blood vessel growth and repair.

## Notes

- Whether a value is good or bad depends on the person, so it is flagged either way.
- Serum values are higher than plasma values.
- This page is general information, not medical advice. Discuss results with a clinician.
