## What it measures

Nerve growth factor supports the survival and repair of nerve cells, especially those that carry pain and touch.

## Reference range

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Abbreviation | NGF |
| Group | Nerve and blood vessel |
| Unit | pg·mL⁻¹ |
| Healthy range | 20–80 |
| Mean | 50 |
| SD | 15 |
| Direction | ↓ Bad — a lowered value is the concern. |

The mean is `(low + high) / 2` and the SD is `(high − low) / 4`, so the healthy range covers Z from −2 to +2.

## Reading the result

- **High** — Not the concern this panel tracks. High levels are linked to some pain conditions.
- **Low** — Linked to poorer nerve maintenance and repair.

## Notes

- Blood levels are low and assays differ. Compare values from the same lab.
- This page is general information, not medical advice. Discuss results with a clinician.
