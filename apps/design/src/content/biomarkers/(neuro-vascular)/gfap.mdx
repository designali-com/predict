## What it measures

Glial fibrillary acidic protein is found in astrocytes, the cells that support nerve cells. It reaches the blood when astrocytes are injured or activated.

## Reading the result

- **High** — Points to brain injury or astrocyte activation. It rises after concussion and in some neurodegenerative diseases.
- **Low** — Not a concern.

## Notes

- Levels rise with age.
