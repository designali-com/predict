## What it measures

Endothelin-1 is made by the cells that line blood vessels. It tightens vessels and helps control blood pressure.

## Reference range

The mean is `(low + high) / 2` and the SD is `(high − low) / 4`, so the healthy range covers Z from −2 to +2.

## Reading the result

- **High** — Points to stressed or damaged blood vessel lining. It is linked to high blood pressure, heart failure, and kidney disease.
- **Low** — Not a concern.

## Notes

- Most endothelin-1 acts locally, so blood levels only partly reflect what happens in vessel walls.
- This page is general information, not medical advice. Discuss results with a clinician.
