## What it measures

Triglycerides carry energy from food and the liver to tissues. Levels rise after meals and with excess sugar, alcohol, and insulin resistance.

The mean is `(low + high) / 2` and the SD is `(high − low) / 4`, so the healthy range covers Z from −2 to +2.

## Reading the result

- **High** — Linked to insulin resistance, fatty liver, and heart risk. Very high levels, above 500 mg·dL⁻¹, can cause pancreatitis.
- **Low** — Not a concern.

## Notes

- Best measured fasting, as a meal raises it for hours.
- This page is general information, not medical advice. Discuss results with a clinician.
