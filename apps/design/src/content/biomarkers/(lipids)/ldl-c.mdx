## What it measures

LDL carries cholesterol to tissues. When there is too much, it lodges in artery walls and builds plaque.

## Reference range

The mean is `(low + high) / 2` and the SD is `(high − low) / 4`, so the healthy range covers Z from −2 to +2.

## Reading the result

- **High** — Linked to a higher risk of heart attack and stroke. The higher it is and the longer it lasts, the higher the risk.
- **Low** — Not a concern.

## Notes

- Targets are lower for people at high cardiovascular risk, often under 70 or 55 mg·dL⁻¹.
- Often calculated rather than measured. The calculation is less accurate when triglycerides are high.
- This page is general information, not medical advice. Discuss results with a clinician.
