## What it measures

The sum of cholesterol in LDL, HDL, and other lipoproteins. Cholesterol is needed for cells and hormones, but too much in the blood builds up in arteries.

## Reference range

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Abbreviation | TC |
| Group | Lipids |
| Unit | mg·dL⁻¹ |
| Healthy range | 120–199 |
| Mean | 159.5 |
| SD | 19.75 |
| Direction | ↑ Bad — a raised value is the concern. |

The mean is `(low + high) / 2` and the SD is `(high − low) / 4`, so the healthy range covers Z from −2 to +2.

## Reading the result

- **High** — Linked to a higher risk of heart disease. Look at LDL and HDL to see which part is raised.
- **Low** — Not the concern this panel tracks.

## Notes

- A high HDL can raise total cholesterol, so it is less useful on its own.
- This page is general information, not medical advice. Discuss results with a clinician.
