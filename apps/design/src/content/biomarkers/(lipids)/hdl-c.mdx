## What it measures

HDL carries cholesterol from tissues back to the liver. Higher levels are generally linked to lower heart risk.

## Reference range

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Abbreviation | HDL-C |
| Group | Lipids |
| Unit | mg·dL⁻¹ |
| Healthy range | 40–80 |
| Mean | 60 |
| SD | 10 |
| Direction | ↓ Bad — a lowered value is the concern. |

The mean is `(low + high) / 2` and the SD is `(high − low) / 4`, so the healthy range covers Z from −2 to +2.

## Reading the result

- **High** — Not the concern this panel tracks.
- **Low** — Linked to a higher cardiovascular risk, especially with high triglycerides and insulin resistance.

## Notes

- Very high HDL does not always protect more.
- Exercise and stopping smoking tend to raise it.
- This page is general information, not medical advice. Discuss results with a clinician.
