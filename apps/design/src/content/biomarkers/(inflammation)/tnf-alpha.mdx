## What it measures

Tumor necrosis factor-α is released mainly by immune cells. It coordinates the inflammatory response and, at high levels for long periods, contributes to tissue damage.

## Reading the result

- **High** — Suggests chronic inflammation. It is linked to autoimmune disease, insulin resistance, and muscle loss.
- **Low** — Not a concern.

## Notes
