## What it measures

Interleukin-6 is a cytokine released by immune cells, fat tissue, and working muscle. It tells the liver to make CRP and other acute-phase proteins.

The mean is `(low + high) / 2` and the SD is `(high − low) / 4`, so the healthy range covers Z from −2 to +2.

## Reading the result

- **High** — Points to active inflammation. Long-term raised levels are linked to ageing, frailty, and cardiometabolic disease.
- **Low** — Not a concern.

## Notes

- Exercise raises IL-6 for a few hours. Test at rest.
- Results vary between assays, so compare values from the same lab.
- This page is general information, not medical advice. Discuss results with a clinician.
