## What it measures

Transforming growth factor-β1 drives wound healing and calms the immune system. When it stays high, it drives scarring, or fibrosis, in organs.

The mean is `(low + high) / 2` and the SD is `(high − low) / 4`, so the healthy range covers Z from −2 to +2.

## Reading the result

- **High** — Linked to fibrosis in the liver, kidney, lungs, and heart.
- **Low** — Not the concern this panel tracks.

## Notes

- Platelets hold a lot of TGF-β1, so serum values are much higher than plasma values.
- This page is general information, not medical advice. Discuss results with a clinician.
