## What it measures

Tissue inhibitor of metalloproteinase-1 blocks MMPs, the enzymes that break down tissue. The balance between the two keeps tissue remodelling in check.

## Reading the result

- **High** — Not the concern this panel tracks. High levels are linked to fibrosis.
- **Low** — Means less control over MMPs, so tissue breakdown can outpace repair.
