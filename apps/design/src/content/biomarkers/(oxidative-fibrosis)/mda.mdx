## What it measures

Malondialdehyde forms when free radicals damage fats in cell membranes. Its level reflects how much oxidative damage is happening.

## Reference range

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Abbreviation | MDA |
| Group | Oxidative stress and fibrosis |
| Unit | µmol·L⁻¹ |
| Healthy range | 1–3.5 |
| Mean | 2.25 |
| SD | 0.625 |
| Direction | ↑ Bad — a raised value is the concern. |

The mean is `(low + high) / 2` and the SD is `(high − low) / 4`, so the healthy range covers Z from −2 to +2.

## Reading the result

- **High** — Suggests oxidative stress, as seen with smoking, poorly controlled diabetes, and chronic inflammation.
- **Low** — Not a concern.

## Notes

- The common TBARS test also picks up other substances, so results vary by method.
